# Port Surfing to the Rust audio player (sangoku-9z6)

_2026-11-08 10:17:45_

## Context

`sangoku-9z6` (feature, P1) asked to translate the supplied three-channel
Surfing score into Rust, reuse the audio player from `sangoku-ais`, support
tempo 160 and wave instrument 0, verify synchronized intros and loops, and keep
Bike Riding playback unchanged.

## Case

The player hard-coded Bike Riding's tempo and waveform, so a second song with
tempo 160 / wave instrument 0 could not use it.

## Before

Only the Bike Riding ROM existed.

## After

A standalone Surfing ROM with the complete three-channel score. Both ROMs build
at opt `0` and `z`. Timing checks pass for both songs: Surfing's first voice
keeps its 195-frame intro versus 180 for voices 2 and 3, and all loops are 1440
frames.

## How

Tempo and waveform selection moved into song configuration; Bike Riding keeps
tempo 144 / instrument 3. Files: `examples/surfing.rs` and
`examples/audio/surfing.rs`. Surfing commands added to the audio concept page
(`wiki/concepts/audio.md`); README unchanged. Formatting, clippy, LSP
diagnostics and whitespace checks pass. Closed `sangoku-9z6`.

## Alternatives

None recorded.
