# 7qz.19: i8 overflow ops (sangoku-7qz.19)

_2026-11-06 19:33:34_

## Context

`cg_clif` emits `uadd/sadd/usub/ssub/umul/smul_overflow` for
`checked_*`/`overflowing_*` and debug overflow checks, so `core` cannot compile
without them; they were unsupported at every width. `sangoku-7qz.19` (P0) asks
for i8/i16/i32 via carry/overflow flags or compare sequences. This log covers
i8.

## Case

`overflowing_add` and friends on `i8`/`u8`.

## Before

Unsupported; error text not recorded.

## After

All six lower and match host `overflowing_*`.

## How

- u/s add/sub overflow lower from `Add8`/`Sub8` plus compares: unsigned flag is
  `sum<lhs` (add) or `lhs<rhs` (sub); signed flag is bit 7 of `(a^r)&(b^r)` for
  add and `(a^b)&(a^d)` for sub, tested with `slt 0`.
- u/smul overflow widen to i16 (zero or sign), call `GbcMul16`; flag is high
  byte `!= 0` or `!=` the sign fill of the low byte.
- Test `i8*overflow_ops_match_host` compares all six against `overflowing_*`
  over 11 edge values squared.
- The LSP index for `lower.rs` was stale (old line numbers, no hits on new
  code), so `cargo build` was the check.

## Alternatives

i16/i32 still open under `7qz.19`.
