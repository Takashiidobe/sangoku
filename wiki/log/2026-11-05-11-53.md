## Files and implementation

`cranelift/codegen/src/isa/lr35902/lower.rs` now maps I16 into two byte
registers. Addition/subtraction use A for the low byte and carry/borrow into the
high byte; bitwise operations run independently on both bytes.

```diff
+emit_accumulator_op(sink, 0x80, dst_low, lhs_low, rhs_low);
+emit_accumulator_op(sink, 0x88, dst_high, lhs_high, rhs_high);
```

The lowering constructs `ValueRegs::two(low, high)` and `inst/emit.rs` uses
`ADD`/`ADC` and `SUB`/`SBC` to propagate carry. This is bytewise support
designed for the 8-bit CPU, not native I16 arithmetic. It unlocks 16-bit pointer
and integer routines needed by `core`.
