# Fill the remaining i16 operation gaps (sangoku-7qz.18)

_2026-11-06 19:46:09_

## Context

The coverage matrix (sangoku-7qz.1) showed `i16` missing `sdiv`, `srem`, `smin`,
`smax`, `iabs`, variable `ishl`/`ushr`, `sshr`, `rotl`, `rotr`, `clz`, `ctz`,
`cls`, `bitrev`, `smulhi`/`umulhi` and the overflow ops, while `imul`, `udiv`,
`urem`, `umin`, `umax`, `popcnt`, `bswap` and constant `ishl` worked. Rust
`core` uses 16-bit integers everywhere (`usize` is 16 bits here), so
sangoku-7qz.18 (P0) required every `i16` row in `tests/coverage/lower.md` to be
`ok`. It is.

## Case

Any `i16` shift, rotate, bit count, high-multiply or overflow op from Rust, e.g.
`x.rotate_left(3)`, `x >> n`, `x.leading_zeros()`, `a.overflowing_mul(b)`.

## Before

Lowering reported these rows as unsupported in the matrix. Exact diagnostics:
not recorded.

## After

No unsupported `i16` rows remain in the matrix.

## How

- New `Inst::Shift16Op` (kind 0 `sar`, 1 `rotl`, 2 `rotr`) handles constant
  `i16` `sshr` and rotates; rotate counts above 8 flip direction. The old `i16`
  `ishl`/`ushr` arms were folded into one arm for all five ops.
- Variable `i16` counts call `GbcShift16` (`__gbc_shift16` in `wide32.asm`):
  value in `B`,`C`, `A = op<<4 | count&15`, result in `A`,`B`.
- `cls` for `i16` is `clz32(x ^ sar(x,1)) - 17`. `umulhi`/`smulhi` widen to
  `i32` and take bytes 2,3 of `GbcMul32`.
- Overflow ops: add/sub compare against the lhs or check the high-byte sign
  bits; mul goes through `GbcMul32` and a high-half test.
- Tests: `i16_shifts_and_rotates_match_host` and
  `i16_cls_mulhi_and_overflow_match_host`.
