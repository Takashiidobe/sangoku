# Execute Unicode case conversion on target (sangoku-6o2)

_2026-11-09 06:59:02_

## Context

`core` `char::to_lowercase`/`to_uppercase` lowered, but no ROM executed them: a
single `to_lowercase` ROM needed 40067 bytes at opt `z` (`conversions::lookup`
3698, `to_lower` 2230, `CaseMappingIter::new` 1386, binary searches ~2 KB).
`sangoku-6o2` (P3, split from sangoku-30k.2) asked for an emulator fixture
checking upstream results (Ä->ä, ß->SS, ǅ->ǆ, non-cased emoji) once ROMs link.

## Case

Lowercase and uppercase of Ä, ß, ǅ and emoji, plus plain UTF-8 `str` methods,
executed in the emulator.

## Before

No executing fixture; the size of a single case ROM blocked it. Exact failure
text not recorded.

## After

Separate lowercase and uppercase ROM fixtures link at opt `z` and execute
upstream mappings for Ä, ß, ǅ and emoji; plain UTF-8 `str` method execution
coverage passes. Both nextest checks pass; clippy and fmt pass.

## How

Added the two fixtures plus the `str` coverage. The combined case fixture needs
32864 bytes, so directions are split to fit 32 KiB (the bead notes it stays 96
bytes over capacity). Closed `sangoku-6o2` and removed its banking dependency.
Existing wasmtime worktree changes were present before this task.

## Alternatives

A combined fixture is not done; it does not fit 32 KiB.
