`cranelift/codegen/src/isa/mod.rs` routes Z80 triples to the builder:

```diff

+z80 = ["target-lexicon/arch_z80"]
```

The new `isa_builder` constructs target flags and the backend. Using Cranelift's
normal lookup path lets callers select Z80 by target triple rather than adding a
separate target-specific entry point.
