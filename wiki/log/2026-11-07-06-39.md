# Basic split f64 legalization (sangoku-7qz.9.4)

_2026-11-07 06:39:57_

## Context

`sangoku-7qz.9.4` (P0, under the wide-value epic `sangoku-7qz.9`): f64 is 8
bytes, wider than anything the LR35902 registers hold, so the backend could not
even move one. The goal was transport and sign operations only, without wide
value register failures; arithmetic, comparison, rounding and conversions go to
`sangoku-7qz.9.4.1`.

## Case

f64 constants, loads/stores, i64 bitcasts, select, block arguments, direct
calls/returns, `fneg`, `fabs` and `fcopysign`. Fixture
`tests/fixtures/emulate/split-f64.clif`:

```text
function %sign(i16) system_v {
block0(v0: i16):
    v1 = load.f64 v0
    v2 = load.f64 v0+8
    v4 = fcopysign v1, v2
    store v4, v0+16
    return
}
```

## Before

f64 values were not legalized (not recorded: exact error text).

## After

Emulator tests preserve signed zero, infinities, NaN payloads and subnormals at
opt level none and speed.

## How

f64 is represented as two i32 bit-pattern halves in the LR35902 wide-value pass,
reusing the wide-integer splitting. Rebuilt cg_clif and passed the blank-ROM
smoke check. Nextest: 97/98 passed initially; the coverage snapshot failed, was
regenerated, and its recheck passed. Clippy, fmt and LSP diagnostics passed.
Backend change is in `third_party/wasmtime`.

## Alternatives

Not done: arithmetic, comparison, rounding and conversions remain in
`sangoku-7qz.9.4.1`.
