# Compact unsigned u128 overflow multiplication (sangoku-57q.4.2)

_2026-11-07 18:43:56_

## Context

`sangoku-57q.4.2` (bug, P1): `tests/fixtures/clif/wide-overflow-mul.rs` made
CLIF but at opt 0 the linked image needed 57985 bytes, and at opt `z` the
`compiler_builtins` `UMulo::mulo` alone grew past one 16 KiB section (0x43A8).
Neither can fit a 32 KiB ROM.

## Case

Unsigned `u128::overflowing_mul` / checked multiplication.

## Before

Image 57985 bytes at opt 0; one helper over a 16 KiB bank at opt `z`. The
fixture expected a capacity failure.

## After

A 122-byte SM83 runtime with a 38-byte stack frame; the fixture links and
returns 1 in the emulator at opt 0 and `z`.

## How

`runtime/mulo128.asm` adapts SDCC `_mullonglong`: shift/add Horner
multiplication with sticky carry overflow detection (provenance in
`runtime/NOTICE`). cg_clif uses a private three-pointer ABI for unsigned u128
overflow multiplication; the linker (`src/link.rs`) includes the helper only
when referenced. Signed and non-target paths keep their existing lowering.
`tests/mulo.rs` checks 15 boundary and 32 deterministic random pairs, input and
output boundaries and stack restoration. `tools/core_report.py` now counts
shipped runtime assembly definitions. Full nextest passed 150 with one
inventory-report failure; after the fix the core-report test passes. Clippy,
fmt, diff checks and hello ROM build pass.

## Alternatives

Signed i128 still used `compiler-builtins` here; handled in `sangoku-57q.4.3`.
