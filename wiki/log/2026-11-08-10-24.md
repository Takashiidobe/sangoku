# Use typed note pitches in the audio player (sangoku-ah4)

_2026-11-08 10:24:03_

## Context

`sangoku-ah4` (task, P2): note pitches were raw `u8` values with per-score
numeric constants, so invalid pitches were representable and each score
redefined the same numbers. The goal was a shared `Note` enum with no change in
playback.

## Case

`Event::Note` took a numeric pitch checked at test time against a range.

## Before

Raw `u8` pitches, per-score numeric constants, and numeric range checks.

## After

`Event::Note` takes a `Note`. Both committed scores import named pitches such as
`CSharp` and `FSharp`. All note pitches and lengths were compared against `HEAD`
and are unchanged. Both timing tests and both ROMs at opt `0`/`z` pass, along
with formatting, clippy and LSP diagnostics.

## How

Added a shared `repr(u8)` `Note` enum with all twelve semitones. Removed the
per-score constants and the obsolete numeric range checks. Updated wiki audio
guidance. Adapted the pitch imports in the user-provided, untracked
`pokemon_center.rs`, leaving that draft untracked. Closed `sangoku-ah4`.

## Alternatives

None recorded.
