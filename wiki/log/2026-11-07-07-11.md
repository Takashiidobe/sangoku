## How

- i64 `udiv`/`urem`/`sdiv`/`srem`: a 64-step restoring loop; signed operations
  work on unsigned magnitudes and restore the sign. The loop keeps an extra
  remainder carry for divisors with the top bit set.
- Divide-by-zero and signed division overflow trap; `MIN % -1` returns zero.
- `umulhi`/`smulhi`/checked multiplication reuse the four-limb multiplier to
  form the full 128-bit product, with signed corrections to the upper half;
  overflow compares that half with the low half's required extension (zero for
  unsigned).
- `cls` is computed from normalized split limbs and leading-zero counts.
- Extended narrow conditional traps to i16/i32 because optimization folds the
  division guard.
- Added bounded none/speed emulator cases for results, overflow flags and traps
  (`split_i64_remaining_arithmetic_matches_host`). Updated legalization and
  optimization concepts.
