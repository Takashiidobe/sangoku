# 7qz.21: memory offsets and narrow loads/stores

- `fold_offset` in the lr35902 lowering adds an out-of-range offset to the address with Add16 and then uses offset 0, instead of bailing. The old inc/dec-hl sequence is kept for small offsets only.
- Load/Uload8/Uload16 and Store/Istore8/Istore16 share one arm; i32 and narrow forms go byte by byte with fresh scratch regs (see 7qz.16), full-width i16 keeps LoadMem16/StoreMem16.
- uload zero-fills upper bytes; istore stores the low bytes of the wider value.
- sload8/sload16 remain unsupported: they need sign fill, noted on 7qz.3.
- Test: tests/lowered.rs `memory_offsets_beyond_a_signed_byte` runs 8 functions in the emulator (offsets 126-128, 300, -5, -200).
- Matrix regenerated: load.offset, uload*, istore* ok.
