`check`, `core`, `export` and `rebase` subcommands. `check` verifies fork ==

fetches upstream HEAD (there is no master branch upstream) and `git am`s the
patches. `rebuild_toolchain.sh` runs `check` first and a core metadata build
last. The patch applies cleanly to current upstream HEAD (index-only check,
nothing rebased).
