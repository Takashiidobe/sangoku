# 7qz.17: i8 shifts and rotates (sangoku-7qz.17)

_2026-11-06 19:26:31_

## Context

Rust `u8`/`i8` code needs shifts and rotates, but the coverage matrix showed
`i8` lacked them (and bnot, select, imul, div/rem, min/max, iabs, popcnt,
clz/ctz/cls, bitrev, trapnz/trapz and the overflow ops). `sangoku-7qz.17` (P0,
under epic `sangoku-7qz`): every i8 row in `tests/coverage/lower.md` must be ok
or have a documented reason.

## Case

`i8` `ishl`/`ushr`/`sshr`/`rotl`/`rotr`.

## Before

Unsupported at `i8` in the coverage matrix; exact error text not recorded.

## After

Constant counts lower inline through the 16-bit shift instructions; variable
counts call a libcall.

## How

- Constant: rotates copy the byte into the high half (`rotl` uses `8-n`), via a
  separate vreg because both halves are reuse-defs.
- Variable: libcall `GbcShift8` (`__gbc_shift8` in `runtime/wide32.asm`): `B`
  value, `C` count, `A` op, count mod 8.
- Test `i8_constant_shifts_and_rotates_match_host` (fixture
  `emulate/shift-i8.clif`).

## Alternatives

Remaining i8 gaps tracked: cls `.23`, overflow `.19`, trapnz/trapz/mulhi `.20`.
