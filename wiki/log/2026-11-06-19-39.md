# 7qz.20/.23: i8 cls, trapnz/trapz, mulhi (sangoku-7qz.20)

_2026-11-06 19:39:35_

## Context

`cg_clif` uses `trapnz`/`trapz` for assertions and bounds checks (unsupported at
i8, `sangoku-7qz.20`) and `umulhi`/`smulhi` were unsupported at all widths;
`cls` (`sangoku-7qz.23`, P3) was unsupported too, though not needed by `core`
yet. This closes the remaining i8 cells of the coverage matrix.

## Case

`i8` `cls`, `trapnz`/`trapz`, `umulhi`/`smulhi`.

## Before

Unsupported; error text not recorded.

## After

The i8 matrix column is now fully ok. `trapnz`/`trapz` lower to:

```asm
ld a, cond
or a
jr z/nz, +1   ; condition depends on trapnz or trapz
rst $10
```

## How

- `cls i8` = `clz32(zero-extended x ^ (x sshr 1)) - 25`; the `sshr` is a 16-bit
  `ShiftRight16` with sign fill and a copied low byte (reuses `clz32` after XOR
  with sign fill, per the bead).
- `umulhi`/`smulhi` i8 widen (zero/sign) to i16, call `GbcMul16`, return the
  high byte.
- `trapnz`/`trapz` i8: new `Inst::TrapIf` (`A` clobbered), same `rst $10` vector
  as `trap`.
- Tests `i8_cls_and_mulhi_match_host` and
  `i8_trapnz_and_trapz_trap_only_on_their_condition` (fixture
  `emulate/trap-cls-mulhi-i8.clif`).

## Alternatives

i16/i32 remain in `.18`/`.19`/`.20`/`.23`.
