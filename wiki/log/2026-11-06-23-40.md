# 7qz.4: min/max/abs and a signed compare bug

- One arm now lowers smin/smax/umin/umax at i8/i16/i32 via `lower_compare` + `select_by_flag` (shared with select, which now covers i8). `iabs` is (x ^ s) - s with s from SignFill8. i8 `bnot` added.
- Bug found by the new emulator test: the signed Compare8/16 setup used `and $80`, so same-sign operands were decided by the sign bit alone. Now `xor $80` (flip sign bits, then unsigned compare). Affects every signed icmp at i8/i16 and the high byte of i32.
- Test: tests/lowered.rs `min_max_abs_match_host`, fixture tests/fixtures/emulate/min-max-abs.clif; edge values plus 12 seeded LCG samples per width, compared with host min/max/abs.
- Not checked: preservation of unrelated registers, only the result registers.
