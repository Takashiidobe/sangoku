# Build uncommitted Radiohead audio auditions (sangoku-cw2, sangoku-7l4)

_2026-11-08 11:01:09_

## Context

`sangoku-cw2` asked for a bounded four-channel GBC arrangement of the first 20
seconds of the user-provided Let Down MIDI, and `sangoku-7l4` for the same of
Creep, as separate audition ROMs for listening feedback.

## Case

Standalone ROMs arranged from MIDI files kept outside the repository, using
typed Rust score events.

## Before

No Let Down or Creep example.

## After

- Let Down: picked and sustained guitar notes split across the pulses; 142
  sixteenth units at tempo 179 (~19.95s), then silence.
- Creep: monophonic guitar and bass with simplified noise percussion; 124
  sixteenth units at tempo 206 approximate the source 93 BPM and end near
  20.05s. Zero-length guitar gates are repaired to one grid unit.

Both stop after the excerpt and build at opt `0`/`z`. Ten bounded audio tests,
formatting and clippy pass.

## How

Added wiki instructions. The original MIDI files remain outside the repository.
The user requested no commit, so all audition changes remain uncommitted for
listening feedback.
