# Fuse compare and branch (sangoku-i70)

_2026-11-09 16:00:00_

sangoku-i70: new CompareBranch8 (inst.isle, inst/emit.rs, inst/mod.rs,
lower.rs fuse_compare_branch). A brif fed by a unique-use I8 icmp with
eq/ne/unsigned condition emits ld a,lhs; cp rhs; jp cc,taken; jp not_taken
(> and <= use an extra jp z/c). Only A is clobbered. The icmp is not lowered
because its result is never used. Signed, 16-bit and 32-bit compares keep the
boolean path. Harness total 942 -> 868 bytes (2.21x -> 2.04x): max_u8 69->40,
count_eq 77->45, sat_chain 143->130. Default nextest 211 passed.
New compare_branches ROM test checks all six unsigned conditions at opt 0/2/z
(nextest passes). Follow-ups: signed fusion, jp->jr (sangoku-2ea).
