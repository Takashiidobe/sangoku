# 7qz.8.3: soft-float f32 div/sqrt/rounding

- runtime/softfloat32.asm gains `__gbc_fdiv32`, `__gbc_fsqrt32`, `__gbc_fround32`. New libcalls GbcFdiv32/GbcFsqrt32/GbcFround32 plumbed through libcall.rs, module naming, rgbds map and lower.rs.
- fdiv: restoring division of the normalized 32-bit mantissas, 26 quotient bits (first is always 1 after pre-shifting a<b), remainder gives sticky, then the shared `sf_roundpack`.
- fsqrt: digit-by-digit root over a 64-bit radicand with the mantissa in the top half (doubled when the unbiased exponent is odd), 26 root bits plus remainder sticky.
- Rounding works on the magnitude bit pattern: clearing the low f fraction bits truncates, adding 1<<f rounds up and carries into the exponent for free. One libcall with a mode byte (0 trunc, 1 floor, 2 ceil, 3 nearest-even); |x|<1 is special-cased.
- Tests: lowered.rs f32_division_matches_host (all edge pairs + 300 random), f32_sqrt_matches_host, f32_rounding_ops_match_host; bit-exact vs host, NaN by is_nan. Sabotaged the sqrt expectation once to confirm it fails.
- Left: int<->float conversions (8.4), f16 (8.5).
