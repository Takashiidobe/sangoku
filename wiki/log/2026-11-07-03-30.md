# Soft-float f32 div/sqrt/rounding (sangoku-7qz.8.3)

_2026-11-07 03:30_

## Context

Continuing the soft-float runtime (8.2), sangoku-7qz.8.3 (P0) added `fdiv`,
`fsqrt` and `trunc`/`floor`/`ceil`/`nearest` for `f32`, via shift-subtract on
the 24-bit mantissa, tested against the host.

## Case

`a / b`, `x.sqrt()`, `x.floor()`/`ceil()`/`trunc()`/`round_ties_even()` on
`f32`.

## Before

Unsupported.

## After

`runtime/softfloat32.asm` gains `__gbc_fdiv32`, `__gbc_fsqrt32`,
`__gbc_fround32`, bit-exact with the host.

## How

- New libcalls `GbcFdiv32`/`GbcFsqrt32`/`GbcFround32` plumbed through
  `libcall.rs`, module naming, rgbds map and `lower.rs`.
- `fdiv`: restoring division of the normalized 32-bit mantissas, 26 quotient
  bits (first is always 1 after pre-shifting a<b), remainder gives sticky, then
  the shared `sf_roundpack`.
- `fsqrt`: digit-by-digit root over a 64-bit radicand with the mantissa in the
  top half (doubled when the unbiased exponent is odd), 26 root bits plus
  remainder sticky.
- Rounding works on the magnitude bit pattern: clearing the low f fraction bits
  truncates, adding `1<<f` rounds up and carries into the exponent for free. One
  libcall with a mode byte (0 trunc, 1 floor, 2 ceil, 3 nearest-even); |x|<1 is
  special-cased.
- Tests: `lowered.rs` `f32_division_matches_host` (all edge pairs + 300 random),
  `f32_sqrt_matches_host`, `f32_rounding_ops_match_host`; NaN by `is_nan`. The
  sqrt expectation was sabotaged once to confirm it fails.

## Alternatives

Left: int<->float conversions (8.4), `f16` (8.5).
