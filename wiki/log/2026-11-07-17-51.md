# Diagnose retained formatting and ROM capacity (sangoku-3kt)

_2026-11-07 17:51:03_

## Context

On a 32 KiB cartridge, a single `{}` or a panic path that formats its message
can drag in kilobytes of `core::fmt`, and the linker only said the ROM was too
big. `sangoku-3kt` (feature, P1) asked for a diagnostic that names the
formatting dependency path and the ROM size during `build`/`run`, and for
meaningful errors instead of `anyhow` strings.

## Case

`examples/sort.rs` with `values.sort_unstable()` retains formatting through the
slice-range failure helpers (`slice_index_fail`, `new_display`) even though the
application never formats, and does not fit in 32768 bytes.

## Before

An oversized ROM failed at link time with no hint about what was retained.
Errors were `anyhow` values.

## After

Build/run inspect reachable emitted sections after the linker probe and print a
`sangoku::retained_formatting` warning (miette, severity Warning) with the
demangled dependency path, identified formatting-section bytes, total retained
ROM bytes, the 32768-byte capacity and the `probe.map` location. An oversized
ROM then fails with a structured `sangoku::rom_capacity` error listing the five
largest sections, before final linking. Literal formatting arguments and serial
`Write` implementations do not trigger the warning. Hello and primes run without
it.

## How

`src/diagnostics.rs` (new `RetainedFormatting`), `src/error.rs` and
`src/link.rs`; application and test-support `anyhow` replaced by `thiserror`
errors and `miette` reports. Emulator failures keep opcode/address details and
I/O errors keep their sources. Probe-map parsing fixed for singular byte
sections; lazy reachability reused and the diagnostic graph built only for
retained code. Sort remains oversized, so its regression
(`sort_reports_retained_formatting_and_rom_capacity` in `tests/examples.rs`) now
expects the formatting and capacity diagnostics (warning ordered before the
capacity error) instead of execution. Full nextest 134/134; final CLI/example
regressions 12/12; clippy, fmt, LSP and hello ROM smoke pass.

## Alternatives

Vendored `cranelift-reader` keeps its transitive `anyhow` dependency; no
vendored changes. The check inspects emitted dependencies and does not prove a
panic can occur.
