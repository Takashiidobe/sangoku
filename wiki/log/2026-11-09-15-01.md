# inc/dec for constant steps (sangoku-2mv)

_2026-11-09 15:01:33_

sangoku-2mv: iadd/isub by +-1 (8- and 16-bit, either operand order for iadd) lower to new IncDec8/IncDec16 (inst.isle, lower.rs unit_step, inst/emit.rs, inst/mod.rs). Byte: inc r/dec r. Word: inc rr/dec rr when regalloc put the value in BC/DE/HL, else inc lo; jr nz,@+3; inc hi (decrement: inc lo; dec lo; jr nz,@+3; dec hi; dec lo, which sets Z from lo==0 without using A). Operands are use(src) + reuse_def(dst), so no move and no A clobber. Harness total 1153 -> 942 bytes (2.71x -> 2.21x of SDCC): copy_u8 130->53, fill 43->27, strlen16 64->33, count_eq 104->77, sum_u8 104->75, max_u8 78->69, sum_u16 133->117, fib16 40->34; the dq5 regressions on sum_u8/count_eq are gone. Most of the win is the constant registers no longer competing for the 7 registers. Tests: full default nextest 210 passed after the lowering change (before the final unit-test/format-only edits); new emulator tests unit_steps_carry_across_byte_boundaries (word/byte steps across 0xFF/0x00, opt 0/2/z, expects 243) and pointer_loops pass. The lr35902 backend unit tests (cargo test -p cranelift-codegen --features lr35902 --lib lr35902) did not compile at HEAD (missing imports in the emit.rs test module); fixed the imports, added encoding/printing tests, and changed compiles_i16_arithmetic... to add 0x0102 since its +1 now becomes inc. Not done: word inc still goes through a scratch pair then moves back instead of inc de, no-op ld a,a after ld a,[de], ld a,b; or a after dec b (noted on sangoku-1de / i70); other constant adds still use a register (an add a,n immediate form is a possible follow-up); no cycle measurement.
