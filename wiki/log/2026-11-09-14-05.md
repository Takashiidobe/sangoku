# Regalloc head-to-head harness against SDCC

_2026-11-09 14:05:33_

sangoku-otw epic, sangoku-e8v: audit of 5 hand-written kernels showed Rust output 1.2-5.3x SDCC sm83 bytes (sum_u8 100 vs 19, max_u8 100 vs 20, fill 47 vs 15, pressure 66 vs 34, mix16 66 vs 56). Most of the gap is lowering, not regalloc2: pointers are copied into HL for every load, p.add(1)/n-=1 go through A instead of inc/dec, compares build a bool from raw db before branching, jp where jr fits. Allocator-side losses: spill reloads while HL is live cost push hl/ld hl,sp+N/ld r,[hl]/pop hl (abi.rs gives regalloc2 no scratch reg and SP-relative 1-byte slots), back-edge and entry spills in loops, ld d,d. Added benchmarks/regalloc/kernels.{rs,c} (11 kernels), benchmarks/regalloc_baseline.py (bytes via rgbasm/rgblink and sdasgb -l, plus insns/moves/stack/jp counts; --check diffs against benchmarks/regalloc_baseline.json and fails if bytes grow; --update rewrites it) and wiki/concepts/regalloc.md. Baseline total: rust 1190 vs sdcc 426 bytes (2.79x); worst strlen16 8.3x, copy_u8 7.3x. opt 2 and z are identical on purpose (aggressive inlining is needed for size, see sangoku-4we). Children filed: dq5 BC/DE loads, 2mv inc/dec, i70 fused cmp+branch, 2ea jr/layout, e6w cheaper spills, 1de loop shuffles, 98d moves via A, 21y rotates. Not done: no dynamic cycle counts yet (static bytes only), nothing executed in the emulator, no nextest wrapper because it needs sdcc installed.
