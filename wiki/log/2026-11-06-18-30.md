# Phase 3: LR35902 lowering from CLIF fixtures

Issue sangoku-1b9. Adds `sangoku lower <input.clif> [output.s|-]`, backed by the
workspace crate tools/lower (sangoku-lower). It parses CLIF with cranelift-reader,
compiles each function with the lr35902 ISA taken from the file's `target`/`set`
lines, and prints the VCode as RGBDS assembly. No rustc or RGBDS is needed.

The formatting is the existing VCode rewriting from cg_clif's src/rgbds.rs,
included by path so the two paths cannot drift; the libcall symbol table moved
into rgbds::runtime_libcalls and is shared. Phase 4 replaces this formatting.
Saved phase 2 files work as input: repeated headers are dropped and `u0:N`
names resolve through the `; symbol` comments. Hand-written fixtures use
`%name` functions.

tests/fixtures/lower: add16, call, select-branch, shl16-const, rust-call (saved
`sangoku clif` output). Expectations are `; CHECK` lines from
tools/update_filecheck.py. shl16-const is correct (the ld a,b / ld b,c lines are the ABI: args in B,C, return in A,B).
Verified in the emulator by audit-shifts on 2026-11-06.
