# Complete scalar integer coverage through i64 (sangoku-7qu)

_2026-11-07 07:26:45_

## Context

`sangoku-7qu` asked for the scalar integer rows of the coverage matrix to be
complete through i64, with every cell either supported or inexpressible.
Remaining narrow-width work in `sangoku-7qz.19`, `.20` and `.23` closes with it.

## Case

i64 narrow stores (`istore8/16/32`) and signed/unsigned extending loads; i32
`uadd/sadd/usub/ssub/umul/smul_overflow`, `umulhi`/`smulhi` and `cls`; traps and
indirect calls across integer widths. Fixture:
`tests/fixtures/emulate/scalar-integer-gaps.clif`.

## Before

Cells for these combinations were unsupported or unprobed in
`tests/coverage/lower.tsv` (not recorded: exact error text).

## After

Every table cell through i64 is supported or inexpressible. Branch-table non-i32
indices are marked inexpressible; the text parser alone does not reject them.
Rebuilt cg_clif and linked the blank ROM; all 102 nextest tests pass. Clippy,
fmt, LSP and diff checks pass.

## How

- Expanded integer probes (all truncating-store widths, all extending-load
  combinations through i64, traps, indirect calls).
- i64 narrow stores use the low limb; extending loads form the low limb from the
  loaded value and fill the high limb with zero or its sign, preserving byte
  order.
- i32 checked arithmetic, multiply-high and `cls` expand to narrower operations;
  checked arithmetic uses a full 64-bit intermediate and compares its upper half
  with the required extension of the low half.
- Bounded none/speed emulator tests for arithmetic flags and memory boundaries.
  Updated coverage and concept docs.

## Alternatives

Not done: float conversions remain separate work.
