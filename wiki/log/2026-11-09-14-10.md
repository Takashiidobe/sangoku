# Share wide divide and multiply through one memory-ABI libcall

_2026-11-09 14:10:00_

## Context

sangoku-7qz.9.8 (P0). The byte-array `i128` divide/remainder and multiply-high
paths from sangoku-7qz.9.3 expanded inline at every use and cost roughly 4-6 KiB
per standalone operation. The bead asked to move the repeated algorithm bodies
into shared runtime helpers, keep signed/unsigned semantics, divide traps,
reentrancy and bounded emulator tests, and not go back to full-limb SSA loops
with register spills. The helper now exists; size/speed variants and some
callers remain (see Alternatives).

## Case

Any `u64`/`i64`/`u128`/`i128` `/`, `%`, `mulhi` or `i128` multiply in Rust, for
example `a / b` on `u64`.

## Currently emits

Each use expanded inline in the `lr35902` legalizer (byte-loop or limb-SSA), and
`cg_clif` lowered `i128` div/rem to `__udivti3`/`__modti3` from
`compiler-builtins`. Measured caller sizes at opt `z`:

```text
u64 div/rem          3.4 KB
i64 sdiv/srem        5.8-6.4 KB
u128 mul             5.6 KB
```

## Should emit

A call to one of two entry points, `__gbc_wide64` / `__gbc_wide128`, with a
memory context (`a`, `b`, op byte, status byte). Caller sizes at opt `z`:

```text
u64 div/rem          0.57 KB
i64 sdiv/srem        0.58-0.60 KB
u128 mul             1.0 KB
i128 div/rem         1.1 KB (no longer pulls compiler-builtins)
```

The shared helper is 502 B linked in total.

## How

- `udiv`/`urem`/`sdiv`/`srem`/`umulhi`/`smulhi` on `i64`/`u64`/`i128`/`u128` and
  `i128` `imul` lower to a call. Both entries jump to one length-parameterised
  core, `runtime/integer/wide/common/core.asm`, with a reentrant 40-byte frame.
- The helper reports zero divisor and `sdiv` overflow in the status byte and the
  caller traps.
- `cg_clif` now leaves `i128` div/rem as CLIF ops on this target.
- Removed `divide64`, `divide_memory128`, `multiply128`,
  `multiply_high_memory128` and their orphaned byte helpers.
- Fixed `tools/core_report.py`: its provider scan only globbed `runtime/*.asm`,
  so it reported every provider missing after the runtime reorg. Intrinsics
  audit regenerated.
- Tests: `wide_arith` (helper vs host on a value grid for all ops and statuses;
  Rust probes at opt `0`/`z`); `lowered` and `formatting` link the new objects.

## Alternatives

Not done, tracked on the bead:

- No size/speed split yet; cycles are high (154k / 494k worst sampled).
- Caller marshaling still dominates the remaining cost (sangoku-57q.10.9).
- `i64` `imul` and 64-bit overflow multiplies still widen through `i128`.
